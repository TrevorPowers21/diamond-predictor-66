**IN FLIGHT (uncommitted as of handoff): the hit-responsibility / attribution fix.**
- Root problem found in the raw distribution: range means wildly off by position (SS +25,
  2B +21 … LF −17, RF −13; ~42-run gap) because hit-debits were attributed to the `hit_zone`
  RETRIEVER, not the responsible fielder. 73% of hits debit an OF; **28% of those are ground
  balls** that got through the infield → IF under-charged, OF over-charged.
- **Fix built (engine, uncommitted):** attribute hit-debits by trajectory + spray, not
  retriever. GB (<10°) → infield spray lane always (never an OF); air (≥10°) → OF lane; spray
  boundaries calibrated from outs (IF: <−22 3B, −22..2 SS, 2..30 2B, >30 1B; OF: <−14 LF,
  −14..14 CF, >14 RF). **No catchability floor** (preserves credit/debit zero-sum). Range split
  into `range_gb/ld/fb` sub-buckets on each row (liner = noisy bucket, regress hardest — the
  differential regression is a noted follow-up, not yet built). 26/26 tests pass.
- **A full-season re-run was launched to re-check the position means (old vs new)** — results
  pending at handoff (scratch `season_run3`). **DO NOT COMMIT the attribution fix until the
  re-run confirms SS/2B come down and OF climbs toward zero.** If it looks right, commit as
  engine v0.5.0.
